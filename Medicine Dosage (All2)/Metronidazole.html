💊 Metronidazole

Antibiotic / Antiprotozoal
📋 SUGGESTED OPTIONS (doctor chooses):
• Giardia: 15–20 mg/kg/day, TID, 5–7 days
• Amebiasis: 35–50 mg/kg/day, TID, ~10 days
• Anaerobic infections: 30–50 mg/kg/day, TID, max daily up to 2250 mg
• C. difficile: 30 mg/kg/day, QID, 10–14 days
• H. pylori: ~20 mg/kg/day, BID, 10–14 days
⚠️ MEDICAL DISCLAIMER: Educational/reference only. Verify with a qualified physician.
