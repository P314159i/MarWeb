Step 2: Primary Headache Diagnosis (ICHD-3)

Migraine Check (ICHD-3)

Requires ≥5 attacks, 4–72 h untreated, ≥2 features, ≥1 accompaniment

≥5 lifetime attacks, each 4–72 h untreated?

Tension Headache Check (ICHD-3)

Requires ≥10 episodes, 30 min–7 days, ≥2 features, limited accompaniment

No nausea/vomiting, and ≤1 of photophobia/phonophobia?
≥10 episodes, <1/month average, 30 min–7 days each?

Cluster Headache Check (ICHD-3)

Requires ≥5 attacks, severe unilateral 15–180 min, ≥1 autonomic + agitation/alcohol trigger

Restless/agitated during attack?
Severe unilateral orbital/temporal pain, 15–180 min, 1–8/day?
Triggered/worsened by alcohol?
